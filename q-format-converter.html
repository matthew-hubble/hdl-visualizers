TI convention. Qm.n counts m integer bits and n fraction bits, with the sign bit on top: a signed word is m+n+1 bits wide, an unsigned word is m+n. Q15 means Q0.15 — a 16-bit signed word holding −1 up to just under +1.
Written forms accepted. Q15, Q7.8, q1.30, 8.24. Values may be plain or exponential: −0.375, 2.5e-3, 1e6.
